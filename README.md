# PROJECT

My first project was a group-based project completed during the second semester under the guidance of Sir Kodic De Guzman and Sir Julius Faustino. The project was successfully presented during our school exhibit, where we demonstrated our skills in programming, hardware integration, and teamwork.
The NPK Sensor Project is a smart agriculture system developed to measure the levels of nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium (K) in the soil. These nutrients are essential for healthy plant growth, contributing to strong roots, healthy leaves, and improved crop production.

The system uses an NPK sensor connected to an Arduino Uno to gather and display real-time soil nutrient data. By monitoring these nutrient levels, farmers and gardeners can determine the correct amount of fertilizer required, helping reduce unnecessary fertilizer use while enhancing crop quality and productivity.
This project demonstrates how technology can be applied in modern agriculture to provide a more efficient, accurate, and convenient method of soil analysis and nutrient management. Its primary goal is to assist farmers and gardeners in monitoring soil conditions effectively and making informed decisions to improve crop growth and overall farm productivity.
